Long-subdivision edge functions • verification record • November 23, 2026

CIRCUIT MODEL
The project charges one gate for each binary AND, binary OR, and unary NOT. Any input or gate can have arbitrarily many readers. The circuit receives one Boolean per vertex and accepts exactly when both endpoints of an edge are true.

PROJECT KERNEL CHECKS
The disposable VM used Lean 4.36.0, the project's physical circuit definitions, Mathlib and the merged PEqualNP.Outside.L_meta_575 proof. SubdivisionLayout defines the coordinates and graph function. StarForest checks the support and final star-forest lower bound. SubdivisionPath proves the physical one-path reduction and its iteration over all edges. SubdivisionUpper constructs a concrete circuit from any original vertex cover. SevenLayout checks the canonical numbering of actual graph inputs and the equivalence between covering its original endpoints and covering the supplied base graph. The full SubdivisionTheorems module passed in that VM with zero errors and zero warnings. Its six named audits cover the unrestricted circuit lower bound, exact size threshold, attainment, cover-threshold equivalence, seven-edge specialization and explicit reduction from a base graph. The file SHA-256 is 941c0d528e2596d9948b7eea9d33d73d48014e393fa673e28190b3e4b1effae0. The individual compilation logs were collected into kernel-audits.txt. The convenience script was syntax-checked; its complete sequence of nine fresh builds was not rerun together during the session.

ADDITIONAL MATCHING-TOOLCHAIN CHECKS
The pure Mathlib modules PathCoverCost, MixedOutcomes and GirthGrowth were compiled under the same pinned Lean version on the host. PathCoverCost proves the minimum number of chosen internal vertices for every parity and every pair of endpoint choices. MixedOutcomes proves the equality of optimal terminal and endpoint-cover charges and the cancellation of the two gates used to add the disjoint anchor. GirthGrowth checks the integer inequalities for the polynomially growing girth construction. All named axiom reports are contained in kernel-audits.txt.

MATHEMATICAL ARGUMENTS
The full mixed-parity result uses the collaboration's two path-elimination lemmas L-meta-575 and L-meta-576. Its general iteration, the graph identification C(H)=|E(H)|+τ(H)−1 for mixed lengths, the component quotient, and the computational complexity arguments are written proofs. The odd-path result applies the unrestricted physical circuit model directly. The all-cycle corollary additionally uses the fourteen-and-up argument plus the earlier independent finite certificate through thirteen inputs. The publication-priority question remains open.

COMPUTATIONAL CHECKS
verify_mixed.py checked 28 fixed-endpoint path cases and 3,282 graph instances. verify_quotient.py compared all 59,809 absent/odd/even edge labelings through five vertices with direct endpoint minimization. verify_compiler.py exhaustively tested ten constructed circuits on all 253,696 combined inputs and independently enumerated their minimum vertex covers. independent-multigraph-compiler.py checked 2,736 additional multigraphs, including parallel edges of either parity, with an independent endpoint search and 64 Boolean assignments per instance. None of the numerical checks is used in the proof for arbitrary long subdivisions.
