# From the formal Hamiltonian lower bounds to planted witnesses

For a finite family of Boolean witnesses, adjoin one baseline variable, independent zero/one rails per witness bit, and a target polynomial. The polynomial consists of the baseline variable plus the target times the sum of the monomial tags of all accepted witnesses. Selecting one accepted witness with zeros and ones projects this polynomial to the exact target over every commutative semiring. An empty witness set leaves one variable.

`AlgebraicMeta.planted_computes` and `AlgebraicMeta.planted_approximates` carry size bounds through that projection for the dense lower bound's actual arithmetic circuit and formal Laurent-series definitions. `AlgebraicMeta.upstream_planted` takes the two released lower-bound statements as named premises and yields their separate superpolynomial rates for planted Hamiltonian cycle polynomials over every field, including the characteristic-dependent thresholds. The independent proof of those two premises was checked in the upstream release.
