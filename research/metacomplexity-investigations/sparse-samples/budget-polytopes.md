# The polytope of feasible read permissions

Fix the labeled list and gate threshold of the companion construction. For each coordinate a bit offers a third read, and a zero bit limits that coordinate to two. The feasible-mask polytope is the convex hull of the permission bit strings for which a binary monotone formula fits the sample within the threshold. The same polytope results if the error penalty is two gates per mislabeled row.

**Theorem.** For even \(N\ge4\) there are explicit fixed sparse data sets with \(Q=\Theta(N^2)\) examples whose feasible-mask polytope has extension complexity \(2^{\Omega(N)}=2^{\Omega(\sqrt Q)}\). This already holds on a specified face where all permissions other than one per possible edge of \(K_N\) have fixed values. The Boolean decision predicate on that face is polynomial-time computable. The same input variables, rows and read caps suffice when the required formula must compute the whole graph edge function.

**Construction.** Introduce an ordinary Boolean selector \(y_e\) per edge of \(K_N\). At vertex \(v\), enforce exactly one selected incident edge. With incident bits in a fixed order, keep the prefix OR. Each new bit must be incompatible with the previous prefix, and the final prefix must be one. The prefix definition takes at most three width-three clauses per new OR; incompatibility takes one binary clause. Short clauses can repeat a literal to fill three positions. The total size over all vertices is \(O(N^2)\). For each edge add just one switch clause \((\neg y_e,\neg y_e,d_e)\), with its last position enabled precisely by the permission for \(e\). The Boolean system has a solution exactly if the available graph contains a perfect matching.

Use the cycle, triangle and cubic padding construction of the [fixed-sample theorem](fixed-sample-read-permissions.md). The total number of ordinary literal occurrences and clause positions is \(O(N^2)\); padding adds at most one vertex per such constructed vertex. There are \(\binom N2\) switch triangles, giving the matching lower bound on size. The resulting cubic graph, subdivided target, formula coordinates, and example count all have order \(N^2\). Examples can be encoded by their two or three coordinate indices. In bits a direct encoding takes \(O(Q\log Q)\).

**The face.** If the full ambient polytope allows a permission for every formula coordinate, pin all cycle and ordinary record vertices to one, all cubic padding vertices to zero, and every private vertex of the subdivided graph to zero. These are valid coordinate-bound faces. Their intersection is nonempty since the complete graph has a perfect matching. Its extreme points are exactly the feasible 0/1 masks satisfying all pins, as a mixture on a tight coordinate bound uses only points tight at that bound. The switch coordinates are the remaining edge variables. Feasibility there is polynomial-time decidable by a perfect-matching algorithm.

On this pinned polytope, \(\sum_e x_e\ge N/2\) is valid. Equality is attainable. At a Boolean equality point, a contained perfect matching already uses all \(N/2\) available edges. The equality face is therefore affinely isomorphic to the ordinary perfect-matching polytope of \(K_N\). A face of a face is a face, and taking faces does not increase extension complexity when equations are free. Rothvoß proved an exponential lower bound for the latter polytope. The application here is to permissions of the explicit sparse formula instance. [Rothvoß, *The matching polytope has exponential extension complexity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1311.2369).

**A general representation.** Let \(A_n\subseteq\{0,1\}^n\) be a nonempty Boolean family with a polynomial-size nonuniform NP verifier. A family of polynomial-size sparse formula instances has a face affinely isomorphic to \(\operatorname{conv}(A_n)\). Use two permission bits \(u_{i,0},u_{i,1}\) per input position and the monotone predicate that some verified word \(a\) uses only enabled rails \(u_{i,a_i}\). Its NP verifier uses \(O(n)\) extra gates to check these rails. Apply the fixed-sample construction and pin all other coordinates as above. Every feasible mask has at least one enabled rail per position. The face on which the sum of enabled rails is \(n\) has precisely the pairs \((1-a_i,a_i)\) of the accepted words. Project to the one-rails to get the affine inverse. The formula/sample encoding and the permission interpretation are the additional restrictions in this representation.
